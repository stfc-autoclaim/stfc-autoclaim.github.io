How It Works

No settings to learn, no accounts to create. Three steps and you're done.

1

Install the extension

One click from the Firefox or Chrome links above. Takes about 10 seconds.

2

Log in to the STFC store

Open the store and log in the way you always do — nothing changes here.

3

Leave the tab open

That's it. Free gifts get grabbed the moment they show up, even if you're not watching.

Once it's running, the toolbar icon tells you everything you need to know:

Green means it's working

Free gifts are being claimed automatically. There's nothing else to do.

Red means it's paused

Auto-claiming is off. One click on the icon turns it back on.

Keep In Mind

What it doesn't do:
  • Doesn't store or see your Scopely login credentials
  • Doesn't touch any page or tab outside the STFC web store
  • Doesn't purchase or claim paid offers — only free gifts with a "Claim" button
  • Doesn't run when your browser is closed — the store tab needs to stay open

It does its best — but nothing's perfect

Once in a while — during game maintenance, if your login session times out, or if the store is open in two tabs at once — a gift might get missed. Just refresh the page and it picks right back up.
